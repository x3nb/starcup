[Intro –male  distinguished  vocals soulful whispered over wind + chimes]

“I know your works…
You are neither cold nor hot…
Behold, I stand at the door and knock.”

[soft pads, distant taiko heartbeat]

[Verse 1]

We speak Your name but never seek Your face,
We sing the songs but hide our faith.
Hands are raised, but hearts are cold,
Lost in the noise, forgetting the gold.

[shakuhachi echoes – delicate and sorrowful]

[Chorus 1 – DnB Drop]

He says:

I wish you were hot or cold, not fading in between,
Lukewarm hearts cannot be clean.
I’ll spit you out, oh can’t you see,
Love burns or dies — there’s no in-between.

[Hard industrial bass, taiko hits, metallic percussion, wide reverb]

[Verse 2]

You say “Lord, Lord” but you never knew Me,
I knocked at the door, you refused to see.
Love is the fruit, the root, the flame,
Without My heart, you wear no name.

[koto plucks shimmer beneath the verse]

[Bridge – Ethereal / Reflective]

Know Me in prayer, seek My light,
Talk to Me softly in the still of night.
I am the Way — come, walk inside,
My love will burn what sin can’t hide.

[builds with choir pad + glass harmonica + rising snare roll]

[Chorus 2 – Triumphant / DnB Full Drop]

Be on fire, be alive,
My Spirit calls — let your heart revive!
Not by story, not by creed,
But by love that plants the seed.

[industrial and heavenly collide – taiko + bass + choral echoes]

[Breakdown – Spoken / Whisper with Ambient Pads]

“Depart from Me… I never knew you.”

But those who know Me — know love.
Those who love — know the Father.

[soft flute and wind; heartbeat drum fades in]
[Final Chorus – Glory Rise]

Burn, O soul, with holy flame,
Love the Son and bear His name.
Cold or hot — decide tonight,
For He returns like fire and light.

[Outro – Whisper & Wind]

“He who has ears, let him hear…”

Love is the mark of those who know Me.

[fade out with celestial choir and soft mechanical echo — heaven and earth blending]

Style & Instruments:
Ethereal male vocals (warm, haunting, angelic).
Atmospheric intro with chimes, shakuhachi flute, koto, soft choir pads.
Build into DnB section with taiko and metallic percussion, deep wobble bass, delayed snares, reverb swells.
Mood: conviction, love, awakening, urgency.
Tempo: ~115 BPM (half-time verses, full DnB drops in choruses).
