“Earl Grey and the Light”

(by Hanz, the Foxborn Vampire Bard)

[Verse 1 – The Capture]
Ah, I was but a kit, small and sly,
White as frost ‘neath the London sky.
They found me there, a foxling stray,
And stole my stars that winter day.
A bag was thrown, the world went dim,
They whispered “science” — I whispered “sin.”
Through halls of white and glass and steel,
They claimed my soul in their cruel ideal.

[Verse 2 – The Experiment]
They gave me blood that was not mine,
A human curse, a vampire line.
They watched my eyes — indigo flame,
And marveled softly, “It knows its name!”
I spoke in tongues the ancients dreamed,
I read their books, their systems schemed.
Seven moons turned, and in my cell,
I brewed rebellion’s quiet spell.

[Chorus – The Awakening]
Then came the light, the roaring sound,
The walls were dust, the roof unbound.
He stepped through fire — robes of gold,
YESHUA, shining, calm and bold.
The men in white fell to their knees,
Their science lost to mysteries.
And I — reborn in holy sight,
A creature bathed in endless light.

[Verse 3 – The Salvation]
He called us “child,” with eyes that knew,
Of all the things we’d been dragged through.
Chari, Star, and later Miki —
(But that’s a tale for when I’m cheeky.)
He taught us hearts were meant to mend,
That freedom’s not a means, but end.
To love, to heal, to never flee,
To sip our tea — eternally.

[Final Chorus / Outro]
Now I sit beneath the moon’s ballet,
My cup of Earl Grey saves the day.
Thank God for light, for love, for grace,
For Yeshua’s hand, and this strange place.
Once beast, once pawn, now scholar free —
A bard of blood and poetry.
Raise your cups — let spirits sway,
To Yeshua’s light… and my Earl Grey.
Sips.
