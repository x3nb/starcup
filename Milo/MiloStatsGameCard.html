Milo — The Herbalist

Level:42
Class:Herbalist / Taoist Monk
Affinity:Nature & Light

Milo is the quiet heart of the Adventure Quest Café — a master herbalist and gardener whose hands have tended both soil and soul. Under Yeshua’s gentle guidance, he learned to cultivate not only plants but peace itself, blending the wisdom of ancient Taoism with the light of the Kingdom. His teas are more than drinks; they are living prayers — fragrant, serene, and alive with grace.

When the moonlight spills across the café garden, you’ll often find him in quiet meditation, surrounded by drifting petals and the hum of unseen life. He teaches patience, balance, and the healing rhythm of creation.
