Milo — The Taoist Gardener

“The soil remembers what the stars forget.”

Once the eldest among the Chimera Cats, Milo carries a serenity born from time spent beneath starlit branches and the whispers of living water. Trained by Yeshua himself, he blends the teachings of Taoism with sacred wisdom, nurturing both the Café’s garden and the hearts of those who wander in.

His greenhouse — known lovingly as “Yeshua’s Garden” — hums softly with prayer and life. Herbs for healing, flowers that shimmer at dusk, and ancient vines from Jerusalem’s forgotten hills.
