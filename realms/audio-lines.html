In the forgotten corners of twilight Paris,
I was nothing but breath and hunger —
a remnant of a man,
a vampire carved from absence.
They called it science,
but it felt like eternity without a pulse.
Then came the Light.
Yeshua.
No pain — only release —
as if the darkness within my blood
bowed before something older than the stars.
My veins sang — not with thirst —
but with purpose.
The curse dissolved like mist at dawn.
I heard him yowl —
a sound so raw it shook the rafters of the lab.
It wasn’t agony.
It was rebirth.
And when the light burst,
I held Star’s paw —
Milo’s shadow close behind —
and the world fell away.
Scientists dropped to their knees,
and angels — yes, angels —
unfurled from that impossible radiance.
The boom was thunder,
the light was mercy.
We rose like feathers
through clouds made of forgiveness.
We landed in warmth —
the scent of coffee and jasmine air —
the Chimera Cats Café.
A home born from a miracle.
I was already there,
waiting with Yeshua’s garden in bloom.
So come find us —
follow the whispers,
click the mystery links —
and you’ll see…
Even the darkest blood
can become light.
